How to play

The roll

Say what you want to do. Peter picks a stat and how hard it is. Roll that stat's die. If you meet the number, it happens.

If you roll the die's maximum, it explodes: roll again and add. If your first die shows 1, you fail, and nothing can save it.

Skills and chrome

You have three skills, in your own words. When one fits, your die goes up a size, or a d20 gets +2. You can use one per roll.

Each skill has a downside. When Peter offers you the trouble, take it for 1 Entropy or say no.

Chrome works like a skill, but you pay for it with Humanity. You have 4, and at 0 you start to lose yourself. Chrome can also do things a person can't.

Fights

Fights use the same roll, and an enemy acts after each of your actions. When you attack, the amount you beat the number by sets the damage:

BEAT IT BY0–45–910+
DAMAGE123

Bare hands always deal 1, and heavy weapons deal at least 2. Attacks are never easier than Normal. Ending a fight in one move, like a shove into the reactor, is two steps harder. If they never saw it coming, there's no roll.

You have 6 health, and at 0 you're out of the fight. A quiet scene gives back 1. The Med Bay restores it all, but the meter rises by 1.

The meter

ISCO's compute is on everyone's screen. It rises with every natural 1 and every station hour. Sabotage drains it.
